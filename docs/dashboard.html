1. Rola i jej wagi

Metric weights and token-share defaults are expert heuristics for model selection. They are not measurements of role performance and do not imply that a higher-weighted benchmark causes better outcomes in that role. The v2 DEV/REVIEW/TEST metric triples are starting points, not empirical truth.
